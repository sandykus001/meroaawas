About Us

Mero Aawas Design And Builders Pvt. Ltd. is a newly established and rapidly growing construction and design company in Nepal, founded in 2025 with a clear vision of delivering modern, reliable, and cost-effective building solutions. The company is committed to providing high-quality construction services that combine innovative design, technical expertise, and practical execution to meet the diverse needs of its clients. With a strong focus on residential and commercial projects, Mero Aawas aims to create functional, durable, and aesthetically pleasing structures while maintaining strict standards of quality, safety, and transparency. The organization emphasizes efficient project management, timely delivery, and client-centric service, ensuring that every project is executed with precision and professionalism. Driven by a team of skilled engineers, designers, and management professionals, Mero Aawas Design And Builders Pvt. Ltd. strives to build long-term relationships based on trust, integrity, and consistent performance, contributing positively to Nepal's growing infrastructure and urban development sector.
